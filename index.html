Common questions

Where is my data stored?

Only on your device. LeafVault has no accounts and no servers, so I can't see or recover your data. If iCloud Backup is on, iOS includes the app's data in your device backup.

How do I move my data to a new phone?

Go to Settings → Back up to a file and save the file somewhere you can reach from the new phone, like iCloud Drive. On the new phone, open Settings → Restore from backup and pick that file.

Can I export my transactions?

Yes. Settings → Export transactions (CSV) creates a file you can open in Numbers, Excel or Google Sheets.

Does changing the currency convert my amounts?

No. It only changes the symbol shown next to amounts.

I'm not getting bill reminders.

Check that reminders are on in Settings → Notifications, and that notifications for LeafVault are allowed in the iOS Settings app.

How do I delete everything?

Settings → Delete all data erases everything in the app. Deleting the app also removes its data from your device.
