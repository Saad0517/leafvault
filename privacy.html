Privacy Policy

LeafVault: Budget & Finance · Last updated November 13, 2026

LeafVault doesn't collect any data. What you enter stays on your device. There are no accounts, no servers, no analytics, no ads and no tracking.

Who I am

LeafVault: Budget & Finance ("LeafVault" or "the app") is made by Muhammad Fahid Khan, an independent developer based in Pakistan. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

Information collected

None. The app doesn't collect, send, sell or share personal data. I never see your transactions, budgets, goals, bills or anything else you add.

The app does not:

  • ask you to create an account or sign in
  • connect to your bank
  • use analytics, crash reporting, advertising or tracking tools
  • use cookies
  • access your location, contacts, camera or photos

Where your data is stored

Everything is stored on your device. If iCloud Backup is turned on, iOS may include the app's data in your device backup. Apple handles that backup under its own privacy policy, and I can't access it.

Face ID

If you turn on the app lock, iOS handles Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode. The app only learns whether unlocking worked. It never receives biometric data.

Reminders

Bill and daily reminders are scheduled on your device. They don't go through any server.

Backups and exports

CSV exports and backup files are created on your device and only go where you choose to save or send them. They contain your financial records, so keep them somewhere safe.

Deleting your data

You can erase everything from Settings → Delete all data, or by deleting the app.

Contacting me

If you email me, I'll use your email address only to reply. If you use Settings → Contact us, the email also includes your device model, iOS version and app version so I can help with problems. You can remove those lines before sending.

Children

The app doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, I'll update this page and the date at the top. If the app ever starts collecting data, this policy and the App Store privacy details will be updated before that version is released.
